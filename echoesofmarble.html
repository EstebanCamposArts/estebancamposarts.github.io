This collection explores the intersection between classical antiquity and contemporary digital expression. It appropriates the iconography of the Greco-Roman bust, a symbol of permanence, history, and idealized beauty, and subjects it to a chromatic and textural reinvention. Each piece fuses the timeless serenity of sculpted marble with explosions of color, glitch effects, and an aesthetic that evokes the energy and controlled chaos of the digital age. The resulting contrast not only revitalizes classical forms but also transforms them into reflections on identity, historical memory, and the human psyche in the context of generative art. The work is a meditation on how the immutable meets the ephemeral, creating 'portraits' that are both historical monuments and futuristic visions.
