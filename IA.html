I present a selection of visual works created through artificial intelligence and generative AI techniques. My intention in making art with AI is to explore the creative possibilities offered by generative models, transforming ideas, concepts, and emotions into images.

Each piece is born from a carefully thought-out and elaborated artistic direction process: from the formulation of prompts and the selection of visual styles, to the final composition and the integration of digital retouching. This practice has allowed me to create a fusion between creativity and expression through words combined with technological power, developing projects that merge aesthetics, innovation, and visual storytelling.
